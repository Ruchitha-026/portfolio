Why this work matters

Context, purpose, and audience

Workshop context

This project was developed while studying the evolution of AI and machine learning. The assignment required a professional artifact that explains how major theories, technologies, expectations, and failures shaped the field.

Purpose and audience

The timeline is designed for classmates, instructors, recruiters, and nontechnical professionals who need a concise overview of AI history without reading a long report.

Problem addressed

AI history contains many dates and technical shifts. A standard chronological list can feel disconnected, so the metro-map structure presents each milestone as part of a continuing route.

Professional relevance

The project shows that I can research complex material, organize it for a specific audience, and create a usable digital experience with clear information hierarchy.

Skills and learning outcomes

Explain key transitions from symbolic AI to machine learning, deep learning, transformers, and generative AI.
Connect technical progress with changes in data, computing power, investment, and public expectations.
Apply responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to an interactive educational experience.
Use accessible buttons, readable contrast, source transparency, and reduced-motion support.

Evidence and process

Research-to-design workflow

I selected and verified major milestones, grouped them into development eras, wrote concise summaries, and then translated the material into an interactive metro line. Expandable stations keep the main view simple while preserving details about people, organizations, impact, and historical context.

Student reflection

Reflection and self-assessment

Creating this timeline helped me understand that a professional artifact should do more than repeat information. It should organize information for a particular audience and make the main ideas easy to explore. I selected a metro-line format because AI history includes periods of rapid progress, pauses, renewed investment, and important transfer points. The connected stations communicate that AI did not develop through one straight technical path, and they made the history easier for me to understand as well.

The strongest part of this artifact is the combination of research, interaction, and audience awareness. I learned to balance detail with readability by hiding longer explanations until the viewer chooses a station. I also considered responsive design, keyboard interaction, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and clear sources. In a future revision, I would add filters and search so a reviewer could compare milestones by technology, organization, or ethical impact.
